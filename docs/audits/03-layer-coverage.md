# 03. Layer Coverage

We model the architecture elements across 10 semantic layers:

- **Ecosystem**: Participant roles and actors.
- **Strategy & Governance**: Supervisory authorities and regulatory Baselines.
- **Business Capability**: Core capability segments.
- **Value Stream**: Previsional Value Chain.
- **Service Landscape**: Service Domains and operations.
- **Business Behavior**: Sequence diagram scenario flows.
- **Information**: Business Objects and structures.
- **Control & State**: Control Records and state machines.
- **Regulation & Control**: CONSAR obligations.
- **Evidence & Measurement**: Auditor audit logs.
